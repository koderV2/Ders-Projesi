Character Info & Lore

Alpha

Alpha lore: The forefront warriors, often seen at the center of attention. They lead with raw strength and inspiring presence.

Strength: High
Intelligence: Low
Magic: Limited

Strengths: High leadership skills, inspiring presence, master of melee combat.

Weaknesses: Often targets of enemy attention, can be impulsive, limited magical ability.

Beta

Beta lore: Strategic thinkers and adapters. They utilize multiple tools and methods to solve problems and overcome threats.

Strength: Medium
Intelligence: High
Magic: Mastered

Strengths: Expert tacticians, able to master multiple forms of magic and technology, excellent at finding alternate routes.

Weaknesses: Can overanalyze, lower base physical endurance, dependent on correct preparation.

Gamma

Gamma lore: Resourceful and connected to the land. They harness natural resources and adapt their environment to their advantage.

Strength: Low
Intelligence: Medium
Magic: Connection to Nature

Strengths: Master of resource extraction and building, strong connection to natural magic, can adapt to diverse environments.

Weaknesses: Lower direct combat capability, vulnerable without defensive structures, can get bogged down in logistics.
