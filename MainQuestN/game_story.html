Game Story

The world is under invasion by creatures from different dimensions. There are three races fighting for the world: Alphas, Betas, and Gammas.

All other races outside of these three have been subjected to genocide and erased from existence. A hero who emerges from among the remaining three races will embark on a journey to close the dimensional gates.

The fate of the world rests in this hero's hands.
